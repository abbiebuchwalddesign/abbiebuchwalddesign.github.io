Work

Abbie assembled a collection of her strongest projects for a showcase exhibition at the end of her time at Lesley University. She exhibited six projects while directing the exhibition for a class of twenty designers. She chose to showcase her fractals as vinyl wall decals as opposed to a static poster version to give the project more fluidity and motion. A Great Day in Boston was showcased as a “record cover” with records coming out, and brand guideline booklets available on the interactive board beneath. Queersources was showcased in a way in which individuals could interact with the content and flip through the posters, and a brochure with information about the web-based portion of the project was also displayed alongside a typeface identity poster.
